# Warehouse confirmation — completed 9 November 2026

This release reports the **completed four-agent warehouse block** of the frozen
M3 protocol: 160 trained policies,20 fresh training-seed clusters, 12,800 evaluation
episodes. Each family retrains its two development-selected settings for 524,288
joint steps per policy. Every profile was also trained; new worlds are not
unseen-profile or external-domain validation.

**Direct has no resolved advantage over the tuned Fixed family.** Its observed
area is larger than the selected Lagrangian pair and smaller than the shielded
pair. These are finite-grid, within-domain results, not general method rankings.
The Fixed interval containing zero is not equivalence. Confirmation doubles the
development per-policy step budget as well as using fresh seeds; the changed
ranking cannot be attributed solely to seed variability.

The endpoint is the area generated by the two frozen candidate points *inside
each training cluster*, with institutional cost and delivery utility both
normalized by agent decisions and a predeclared cost bound 0.05. It is not the
area of mean points or an implemented policy-switching controller. Plotting
units differ deliberately: costs per 1,000 agent decisions; deliveries per 1,000
joint steps. All 20 seed points, 160 policy count points and 60 paired differences
are retained. Cost-bound sensitivity keeps the same selected pairs.

**Inference remains DESCRIPTIVE_PARTIAL_FAMILY.** The original global Holm12
family has three warehouse, three RWARE learning and six controlled-execution
contrasts. Nine remain pending. No adjusted p-values or rejection decisions are
reported. Bootstrap and paired-t intervals are pointwise, not simultaneous.
Exact sign-flip tests require the paired joint sign-invariance null; they are
not distribution-free tests of mean zero alone.

Shield-aware structurally eliminates correction by conditioning proposals on
admissibility. Its deployment adds 625 policy-oracle rows per joint step, excluding
the common monitor and evaluator-only diagnostics. Direct uses an exact cost
table in training and deploys a product actor. Environment budgets do not
equalize oracle information or computation. No proof-work saving, latency gain,
external RWARE transfer, trained sampled-estimator result or larger-agent
advantage is established here.

## Files and checks

- `PUBLIC_RESULT.json` is an explicitly sanitized **projection** of the original
  frozen output. It retains exact rational endpoints and all contrast statistics,
  binds the original output's SHA256, and omits private receipt/path provenance.
  It is not interchangeable with the original source-bound inference packet.
- `SOURCE_BINDINGS.json` links the unchanged 43 study source files and frozen
  analysis implementation already published elsewhere in this repository.
- CSVs expose candidate, seed, family, profile and paired-contrast data. The two
  SVG figures retain individual training clusters. Sensitivity CSVs are
  descriptive and do not select a replacement primary bound.
- `MANIFEST.json` records byte length, SHA256 and Git blob identity for each
  released file and the standard-library verifier.

From the repository root run:

```bash
python3 scripts/verify_warehouse_confirmation.py
```

The verifier checks source/file identities and independently reconstructs the
80 two-point areas, 60 paired differences, means, sample variances and all three
inclusive exact 2^20 sign tests from the 160 published policy counts. It starts no
training, proof generation, network connection or remote job. It does **not**
replay raw policies/traces, re-create the bootstrap PCG64 streams, attest a data
publisher, or validate the simulator against a physical warehouse. The complete
original retained-record audit and local independent 12,800 case-summary
reconstruction are bound by the original packet and archived separately.
