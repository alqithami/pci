# PCI: scientific narrative and evidence boundaries

**Updated 9 October 2026.** This public research narrative separates completed studies, later statistical interpretation, two-agent circuit validation, and an ongoing post-submission benchmark. It is not a submitted rebuttal, a confidential review transcript, or a new manuscript upload. The frozen scientific sources and existing result extracts remain unchanged.

## Central contribution

PCI makes three interfaces explicit: a versioned institution specifies operational predicates and contextual overlaps; policies propose actions under those rules; an admission and evidence contract binds a verified relation to the expected corrected action and interaction. Contextual regularization is optional. Neither agreeing heads nor an authentic signed statement alone establishes the concrete joint predicate.

The implemented within-agent sheaf reduces to projected expected-feature matching with fixed, state-instantiated maps. That specification is useful for identifying what is compared, but is not a new inter-agent sheaf solver or a general convergence theorem. The proof establishes the encoded transition relation under authentic-state, correct-key and faithful-execution assumptions. It does not prove policy inference, correction optimality, task desirability, or institutional legitimacy.

## Completed learning evidence and corrected inference

The base contains 110 trained policies and 12 controller evaluations; the follow-up contains 125 policies with normalized mechanisms, a fixed nonsemantic rotation, an adaptive PPO-Lagrangian implementation and a documented RWARE extension. The studies have different objectives and seed sets and are not pooled as replications of one algorithm. RWARE is an independent warehouse implementation, not a second real-world domain or an unmodified benchmark configuration.

The original bootstrap comparisons are retained in [base](BASE_RESULTS.md) and [follow-up](FOLLOWUP_RESULTS.md) results. The later [complete-family sensitivity analysis](../analysis/nominal_sensitivity_20261009/) adds exact event totals and reproducible calculations for all 40 nominal endpoint comparisons. Neither the separately Holm-adjusted paired-t family nor the sign-flip family contains p<0.05. The RWARE-tiny rotation interval changes from bootstrap [0.23, 3.32] to paired-t [-0.75, 4.07]. This does not erase the measured difference; it prevents presenting it as confirmatory semantic superiority.

Fixed penalties are not generally beaten. The reservation auction has stronger open-layout means on both reported axes, and the adaptive comparator has the highest mean RWARE-tiny throughput at different proposal compliance. Its composite cost target is not a joint-action failure probability; recorded multipliers did not reach their upper cap. Five training seeds, one rotation draw, unmatched realized gradient strength and finite-budget learning remain attribution limits.

All 1,200 nominal unshielded RWARE episodes record no quota or capacity violations. High nominal compliance does not demonstrate resolution of active institutional conflicts. Tight-quota outcomes provide exposure but are secondary exploratory analyses, not a substitute primary hypothesis.

## Completed certification is not a privacy theorem

The original-protocol follow-up contains twelve 256-step episodes, 3,072 proof-gated transitions, 180 deliveries, 36 counter resets and six rule-version changes. All 3,216 saved SNARK proofs were checked after the run, together with commitments and an independently written scalar transition oracle. Those internal checks reuse the proof libraries; they are not an external security audit. Signature bytes were not retained in that study.

At eight agents, 155–176 replacements are individual components among 2,048 decisions (7.57–8.59%). The separately counted 74–88 affected joint steps are 28.91–34.38% of an episode. Physical corrections are included in those original counts. Certificates attest admitted actions, which can differ from policy proposals.

The original public action commitment used zero salt. With four five-action agents, 625 candidate joint actions can be enumerated for the public interaction fields. A public nonce does not hide that finite dictionary. Recovered movement and known initial state can expose positions. This is public-input leakage, not a break of the proof system's zero-knowledge property. General trajectory confidentiality is not established.

The [separate repair](../experiments/PCI_Blinded_Action_Prototype_v2/) adds a private action opening and commitment domain 1004, with new keys. It passed two-agent functional tests before the post-submission benchmark. Those tests do not inherit the old protocol's full-episode measurements, prove hiding against all auxiliary information, or authenticate sensors.

## What the ongoing study will assess

The [post-submission protocol](../experiments/PCI_Postsubmission_Certified_v1/protocol.json) fixes 270 complete episodes across three frozen policy families, five archived training seeds, populations 2/4/8, three rule regimes, and two new evaluation seeds. It tests the repaired relation without retraining those policies.

The relevant questions are whether the policies differ in useful throughput and additional institutional corrections under the same gate, and what assurance and cost each evidence interface provides. The experiment separates physical corrections from further institutional corrections and compares Groth16, PLONK, signed assertions and authenticated disclosure on matched records in balanced order. A signed assertion requires trust in the signer's predicate evaluation; disclosure permits predicate reevaluation; a SNARK permits relation checking against authentic expected commitments without receiving private openings through that interface. Signer-key authentication remains an external trust assumption.

Private reproduction records and disclosed-record views are not the SNARK-only auditor view. Runtime metadata and auxiliary information remain relevant. The design retains signatures/public keys and requires per-episode proof/signature replay. Its primary operational family has 36 contrasts, distinct from the completed learning analysis's 40 contrasts.

**No pending performance result is included here.** The [dated status snapshot](POSTSUBMISSION_STATUS.json) records progress only. The run reuses five old seeds; it is not the proposed fresh-seed, multiple-map confirmatory learning study, a GPU training run, or a recursive episode-proof implementation.

## Paper-development work that does not require the new outcomes

The institutional definitions, policy/admission distinction, leakage qualification, alternative assurance assumptions, metric denominators and study chronology can be finalized now. The [evidence-to-section checklist](PAPER_EVIDENCE_MAP.md) identifies the exact output files and gates needed before filling in a future results section. It provides no invented numeric placeholders.

Preserve every fixed comparator, failed attempt and zero-delivery episode. Keep original and repaired circuit identifiers and timing scopes separate. Distinguish functional tests, complete-data analysis, archive verification and independent scientific assessment. Mathematical results must address the actual pooled policy and their stated assumptions; more propositions or more proof calls alone do not establish novelty.

The remaining learning program requires a separately fixed sample-size/precision plan, independent map draws, comparable operating-point selection, active-rule training and appropriate reference baselines. A non-warehouse domain and a nontrivial compatibility-to-admission result remain separate research directions. None is reported as complete or automatically launched by this repository release.
