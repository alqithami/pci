# PCI: revised scientific story and response-to-concerns map

**Evidence cutoff: 8 October 2026, after the completed follow-up audit.** This public development narrative is not a submitted rebuttal or reproduction of confidential reviews. Original and follow-up studies remain distinct; no scientific source, parameter or archived outcome is changed by this documentation update.

## Central argument

Agents governed by overlapping institutional contexts face three questions: whether contextual decisions agree on shared quantities, whether a concrete joint action satisfies operational rules, and what evidence establishes that satisfaction. PCI provides separate computational interfaces: decision-producing context heads regularized on declared overlap effects, an operational transition predicate, and a certificate bound to the expected interaction before execution.

An institution specifies contexts, maps, versioned predicates and an evidence/admission contract. Coordination is the policy or planner operating within that specification. Cryptographic verification is neither the coordination algorithm nor evidence that a low consistency score guarantees safety. Agreeing context heads can still generate incompatible joint resource demands. An access quota is a specified allocation rule, not a theorem of distributive fairness.

## Base evidence remains visible

The base contains 110 trained runs and 12 controller evaluations over three custom layouts, with five training seeds. Its audit reconstructs 35,136 final-policy/controller rows and paired intervals. An independent oracle checks 244 saved admitted traces, totaling 62,464 transitions; selected cross-platform replay covers 24 episodes. These checks do not imply universal algorithmic validity.

PCI's base throughput intervals against consensus contain zero on all three layouts; its compliance improvements versus penalties accompany throughput reductions. Shuffled-map comparisons do not isolate a robust throughput benefit. The bounded 96-transition certificate study uses real proofs but does not cross a quota window. Its full results remain in [BASE_RESULTS.md](BASE_RESULTS.md) and are not replaced by the follow-up.

## Completed follow-up: what changed and what the results mean

The follow-up was designed after the base outcomes were inspected. Its fixed [protocol](../experiments/PCI_AAMAS27_Followup_v1/docs/PROTOCOL.md) specifies new seeds 101–105, normalized variants, a spectrum-matched nonsemantic control and an empirical-rate PPO-Lagrangian adaptation. RWARE uses unchanged native dynamics with explicitly common task/map features, shaping and rules; the adaptive comparator is not an exact published MACPO implementation.

All **125 runs, 36,000 final-policy episodes and 5,000 checkpoint-evaluation episodes completed**. Independent reconstruction reproduces all 1,680 paired and 2,100 condition intervals to absolute tolerance 1e-12. The follow-up's 18,663 archived payloads pass independent hashes. All 1,945 zero-delivery evaluation episodes remain included.

**Localized positive evidence:** on RWARE tiny, normalized PCI improves throughput by 2.67 [1.76, 3.58] deliveries per 1,000 steps versus normalized consensus and 1.66 [0.23, 3.32] versus the nonsemantic control. It does not establish a throughput improvement over fixed penalties. RWARE small has no established throughput advantage. Custom two doors loses 4.07 [-7.52, -0.94] deliveries versus consensus while gaining 1.66 percentage points [0.15, 2.80] of compliance. Other custom effects remain mixed. All intervals are pointwise/exploratory, not multiplicity-adjusted.

**Governance exposure:** all 1,200 nominal unshielded RWARE episodes have zero logged quota/capacity violations. Near-one compliance is not evidence of resolving those active conflicts. Tight-quota RWARE instead records 6,624 unshielded agent-step quota violations. On tiny, throughput gains versus consensus/control persist, while the fixed-penalty comparison shows a compliance gain without an established throughput gain. The external claim is therefore conditional, not broad institutional dominance.

**Complete execution evidence:** twelve 256-step episodes execute 3,072 freshly Groth16-certified transitions and deliver 180 tasks. The audit independently verifies all 3,216 saved SNARKs, commitment/public bindings, all certified transition predicates, 36 resets and six rule-version changes. Paired generation medians are 296–450 ms for Groth16 and roughly 14.3 seconds for PLONK. These costs rule out the old negligible-generation claim. Ed25519 signatures were not archived for independent replay, and no RWARE circuit is claimed.

The detailed [follow-up findings](FOLLOWUP_RESULTS.md) retain all qualifications and point to the presentation extracts. Full raw archives remain author-held, with checksums in [ARTIFACTS.md](ARTIFACTS.md).

## Theory and positioning

A positive-weight sum of squared overlap residuals vanishes exactly when each residual vanishes at a fixed state. A distance-to-kernel bound depends on the smallest positive singular value. These interpretations are not neural-PPO convergence, operational feasibility, or return guarantees. The separate affine diagnostic has a known oracle; high neural training loss is not an infeasibility certificate.

The contribution must be evaluated against institutional/norm-enforcement, sheaf-coordination, constrained-learning and proof-system literature. Existing primitives are used. SIGMA, Sheaf-ADMM, MACPO and the published MAPPO-Lagrangian code are not represented as executed baselines. Priority and state-of-the-art claims require an explicit literature/implementation assessment.

## Current paper narrative

The supported story is **selective contextual agreement with condition-dependent learning benefits, together with an interaction-bound operational evidence contract demonstrated across complete modeled episodes**. This is more informative than either blanket superiority or a proof-verification sample alone. It preserves unfavorable and null outcomes and distinguishes consistency, utility and certified admissibility.

Keep base and normalized follow-up objectives separate; retain every fixed comparator and configuration; average episodes within training seeds; report throughput jointly with compliance/cost/intervention measures. Five seeds remain five independent repetitions. A missing detected difference is not equivalence. Finite implementation audits do not establish cryptographic security assumptions, physical truth, cross-domain performance or final submission readiness.
